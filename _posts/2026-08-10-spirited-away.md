Studio Ghibli is famous for refusing to talk down to children, and
*Spirited Away* is the clearest example of that philosophy. There is no
villain to defeat, no quest to complete, no lesson to underline. Chihiro
simply has to behave — and survive — long enough to save her parents.

## The good

The animation is the obvious draw, but the writing is the quiet miracle.
Every spirit in the bathhouse is its own tiny character, with its own
quirk, and every scene is built around a small act of kindness.

## The slightly less good

If you are not in the right mood, the middle act can feel a little
wandering. There is a train sequence that is gorgeous and slow, and you
either lean into it or you reach for your phone.

## Verdict

A film that trusts its audience completely. Five stars for the bathhouse
scene alone.

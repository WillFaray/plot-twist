The most ambitious movie of 2022, and easily the strangest Best Picture
winner of all time. The Daniels pack so much into two hours that the
only way to describe the plot is "a mother fights the end of the
multiverse using a fanny pack".

## What works

- The emotional core. Underneath every universe, every kung-fu fight
  and every talking rock, this is a film about a mother and a daughter
  who have forgotten how to be in the same room.
- Ke Huy Quan. A performance that earns every single second of screen
  time.
- The score. Son Lux deserves an award of their own.

## What I struggled with

The first act is a lot. The film throws you into the chaos on purpose,
which is the right call, but it took me a full twenty minutes to lock
in.

## Verdict

A weird, generous, exhausting triumph. Bring tissues.

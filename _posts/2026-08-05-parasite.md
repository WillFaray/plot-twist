Bong Joon-ho made a heist movie, a horror movie, a comedy and a tragedy
in a single ninety-minute run, and somehow none of those four films step
on the others.

## What works

- The screenplay. Every line of dialogue is doing two jobs at once.
- The production design. The Park house is a *character*. Every room
  tells you which class belongs there.
- The pacing. The film takes its time setting up the con, and the
  switch to horror in the third act is devastating because of it.

## What surprised me

The more I think about it, the less the film feels metaphorical and the
more it feels like a literal diagram of how late-stage capitalism
functions: the rich float, the poor climb, and gravity only ever points
one way.

## Verdict

Deserved every award it won. The last twenty minutes are a punch to
the stomach.

## What I liked

  ![](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/original/vGmB2zcniZJRv4wX8qsgNofeSMY.jpg)

-  **The animation.** The blend of animated characters and real people was very well executed, avoiding any sense of awkwardness in the interactions and settings. Despite being exaggerated and cartoonish, the Coyote feels like a real being within the context of the film.

-  **The nostalgia.** Seeing so many memorable and nostalgic characters was a breath of fresh air, recalling the jokes and reliving classic moments. And they still delivered plenty of laughs.

And speaking of laughter, we can't forget to mention:

-  **The jokes.** The jokes managed to entertain both adults and children at my screening, even though some were aimed at different audiences. The film avoids stereotypes and delivers well-crafted, well-executed jokes, making it genuinely funny.

## What could be better

- Some scenes and characters seemed to exist solely for the sake of the plot (What's up, Doc?), and I feel that the motivations and fates of some of them could have been better developed and explained.

- The courtroom scenes could have been a bit longer; they were really good.

## Final thoughts

![](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/original/gZpeLrYnjoOfzkuDtmhPPgUYFUd.jpg)  

_Coyote vs. Acme_ is a sharp, laugh-out-loud celebration of classic animation and corporate satire that proves Wile E. Coyote's eternal persistence is as endearing as ever. Packed with genuine heart, clever meta-humor, and inventive slapstick, it delivers a genuinely entertaining ride for both lifelong Looney Tunes fans and newcomers alike. It is a triumphant, inventive comedy that reminds us why these timeless characters continue to resonate across generations.

Wes Anderson at his most Wes Anderson — symmetrical frames, pastel color palettes, deadpan line deliveries — but with a surprising emotional core hiding under all the whimsy. It's a film about friendship, fascism, and the lost art of being a decent person.

## What works

- **Ralph Fiennes.** A career-best comedic performance. His timing is surgical.
- **The nested narratives.** Story within a story within a story, and every layer is distinct.
- **Production design.** Every frame could be a postcard. The model work is exquisite.

## What I struggled with

The rapid-fire dialogue and dense visual gags demand your full attention. Look away for a second and you've missed three jokes.

## Verdict

A confection with surprising depth. Funny, beautiful, and quietly melancholic underneath all those layers of storytelling.

Denis Villeneuve somehow made a sequel to one of the most revered sci-fi films ever and matched it. Every frame is a painting you could hang in a gallery — orange dust storms, holographic billboards, the sterile Wallace headquarters.

## What works

- **Roger Deakins' cinematography.** Oscar well deserved. The Vegas sequence alone is worth the runtime.
- **The silence.** The film breathes. Long pauses, empty rooms, a single figure against an immense landscape.
- **Ryan Gosling.** A perfect blank slate who slowly reveals more than he ever says.

## What I struggled with

The 163-minute runtime is a commitment. The first act drags deliberately, which is either atmospheric or indulgent depending on your mood.

## Verdict

A rare sequel that deepens the original without betraying it. Cinema as world-building at its finest.
